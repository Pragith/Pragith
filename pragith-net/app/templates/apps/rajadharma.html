A strategy game set in a mythic royal court

Rajadharma

You rule a kingdom where every choice has a cost. Each choice affects Dharma, Artha, Shakti and Praja: your duty, treasury, strength and people's welfare.

Created by Pragith Prakash.

Choose with care

You hear requests from sages, royal advisers and mythic visitors. Your answers change the kingdom's resources and the time that passes. Pressure grows throughout your reign; neglect and excess can both bring it to an end.

You discover characters, earn deeds and collect relics. Your local dynasty records let you compare your reigns.
