A mythic court strategy game

Rajadharma

Rule a kingdom where every choice has a cost. Balance Dharma, Artha, Shakti and Praja: your duty, treasury, strength and people's welfare.

Created by Pragith Prakash.

Choose with care

Hear requests from sages, royal advisers and mythic visitors. Your answers change the kingdom's resources and the time that passes. Pressure grows throughout your reign; neglect and excess can both bring it to an end.

Discover characters, earn deeds and collect relics. Compare your reigns in your local dynasty records.
