Title: About Pragith Prakash | AI Forward Deployed Engineer

Findings: C09, C10, C11, C12, C13, C14

I build, operate and explain complex technical systems.

I’m an AI Forward Deployed Engineer, consultant and educator with data and software experience since 2012. My work covers enterprise data platforms, applied AI, cloud infrastructure and the release and support practices that keep them useful.

How I got here

I began in data analysis and backend systems in 2012. Since then, I have worked through enterprise data science, consulting, regulated-data engineering, business intelligence, platform operations and AI system delivery.

The job has expanded with each role. I can move from an architecture discussion into Python or SQL, trace a failing pipeline, review access boundaries, repair a release workflow and explain the trade-offs to stakeholders who do not share the same technical vocabulary.

I work independently when a problem needs concentrated ownership. In enterprise environments, I collaborate with engineering, security, product, domain and governance teams because those systems require more than one point of view.

Read the career timeline

Teaching changed my engineering

I have taught graduate statistics, data visualization, dashboards and real-time Python APIs. I have also led workforce programs in data science and data engineering, and organized developer workshops through Google Developer Groups.

A classroom exposes weak explanations quickly. That experience taught me to document decisions, make operating assumptions visible and leave enough context for the next person to understand a system.

See my teaching work

How I approach the work
Start with the operational problem and the team that will own the result.
Choose the simplest architecture that meets the actual constraints.
Design access control, monitoring and failure handling with the main workflow.
Evaluate model output against representative tasks and known failure cases.
Document decisions so another engineer can operate the system.
What I’m studying now

I started experimenting with software and web technology in 2004. These days I spend that curiosity on self-hosted models, developer tools, distributed systems, cloud economics and the boundary between automated work and human approval.

I direct commercial consulting through Vriksh Consulting Services Inc. This website remains my personal professional record.
